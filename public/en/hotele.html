FAQ

Before we set a date

Every property has a different layout, light and needs, so these answers are a starting point for a conversation. Tell me about your property and I'll answer with specifics.

How long does a hotel or restaurant session take?

Depends on the number of rooms, halls, and whether we also shoot food and staff at work. We plan it in hours or days, depending on the size of the property.

What time of day do you photograph interiors?

Usually at dawn or dusk, that's when light does half the work for me. Sometimes it means coming twice, once for natural daylight, once for the evening mood.

Do you shoot with a drone too?

Yes, if the property benefits from it, like a mountain or lakeside setting. Just mention it when we're quoting and we'll see if it makes sense for your location.

How many photos will we get from a hotel session?

As many as it takes for every important space to have its best shot, usually a few per room or hall. We agree on the scope together, based on how many spaces you want to show.

How is a property with many rooms or halls priced?

I price it by scope, not a fixed per-room rate, a suite with a view and a standard room aren't the same job. Tell me how much space you want covered and you'll get a real quote.

How long until we get the finished material?

We agree on a deadline together at the start, especially if the photos need to go live on your website or booking system by a specific date.
