FAQ

Before we work out the details

Every event has its own dynamic, so these answers are a general picture. Tell me what you're organising and I'll tell you how we'll handle it.

What does covering a festival or concert with you actually look like?

I'm wherever something's happening, on stage, backstage, in the crowd. I know the rhythm of these events from years at Beats for Love and the Opole festival, so I catch moments as they happen, not after.

Do you need accreditation or backstage access?

If the event requires it, yes. Just tell me in advance what access the organiser can provide, and I'll fit in like any other crew member.

How fast do we get the photos after the event?

I know speed matters at events, especially for social media the next day. We set the selection and delivery timeline together before the event, so nobody's left waiting.

Do you shoot video or short reels too?

I mainly work as a photographer, but for some jobs I add video, from a drone or handheld. Tell me exactly what you need and I'll tell you what's possible.

How is a multi-day or recurring event priced?

For longer or recurring events we can work out better terms than for a one-off job. Tell me the scale you're thinking of and I'll put together a real proposal.

Do you travel abroad too?

Yes, I've already worked abroad with a camera, from Ostrava to Athens. Travel and accommodation are billed separately and clearly, never hidden in the rate.
