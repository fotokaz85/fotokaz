FAQ

Before we start the project

Every company and every team has different needs, so these answers are a general picture, not a price list. Tell me what you have in mind and I'll answer with specifics.

How is your brand portrait different from a regular photo session?

I don't do stiff poses against a grey backdrop. I talk to you before the session, so the photo shows who you really are at work, not someone playing themselves for the camera.

How many people can you photograph in one company session?

From a single executive portrait to the whole team. Bigger groups just need more time, we figure out together how many people and roles you want covered.

Do you shoot only people, or also interiors and products?

Both, and often together, a chef's portrait in their own restaurant says more than one against a white wall. Interiors, details, people at work, whatever builds the brand's image.

What are the terms for using the photos, ads, social media, press?

We settle that when quoting, because the scope of use affects the price, using a photo on Instagram is different from a nationwide billboard. Tell me where the photos will appear and I'll set the licence accordingly.

How is a business session priced?

It depends on scope: number of people, location, number of days. I'd rather hear what you have in mind than guess a range, a single portrait session and a campaign for a hotel chain are entirely different budgets.

How long until we get the finished material?

We set the deadline together at the start, especially if the photos are going into a specific campaign or publication on a set date. You'll know exactly when to expect them.
